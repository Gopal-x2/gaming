    Data Protection

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: September 13, 2026

    1. Information We Collect

    We collect personal data required to operate esports tournaments safely:

    • Account Info: Name, Username, Email address, Phone number, and In-Game Tags/IDs.
    • Payment Data: Transaction Order IDs, Payment IDs, and verification statuses via Razorpay (we never store card CVV or banking passwords).
    • Technical Logs: IP addresses, browser types, and session tokens for security auditing.

    2. How We Use Your Data

    • To manage tournament registrations and distribute room credentials.
    • To verify payments securely and process prize payouts.
    • To send transactional emails (registration confirmations, match schedules, room updates).
    • To enforce anti-cheat guidelines and prevent fraud.

    3. Data Security

    Your data is encrypted using standard SSL/TLS protocol during transit and stored in protected database environments with hashed passwords (`bcrypt`).
