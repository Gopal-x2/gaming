    Fair Play & Anti-Cheat Policy

    Last updated: September 13, 2026

    1. Zero Tolerance for Cheating

    Nexus Esports maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy towards cheating, hacking, exploit abuse, and unsportsmanlike conduct.

    Forbidden Actions

    • Third-Party Tools: Using wallhacks, auto-aim, recoil reducers, radar hacks, or modified game binaries (APKs).
    • Emulator Restrictions: Mobile tournaments (e.g. BGMI, Free Fire) strictly forbid PC emulators unless explicitly designated as an Emulator Tournament.
    • Teaming & Collusion: Teaming up with opponent squads or intentionally feeding kills is strictly prohibited.
    • Roster Ringing: Playing under another user's account or submitting un-registered ringers will lead to immediate disqualification.

    2. Penalties & Bans

    Violators will face immediate disqualification, forfeiture of prize money, and a permanent ban across the platform.
