    Refund & Cancellation Policy

    Last updated: September 13, 2026

    1. Tournament Entry Fee Refunds

    Entry fees paid by squad captains for joining competitive esports tournaments on Nexus Esports are generally non-refundable once registration is confirmed, except under the specific circumstances listed below.

    2. Eligible Circumstances for Refund

    • Tournament Cancellation: If a tournament is cancelled by the Admin or platform due to technical failures, unforeseen game publisher server outages, or insufficient participant count, 100% of the entry fee will be refunded to the original payment source.
    • Duplicate Payment: If money is debited twice from your bank account or card due to a payment gateway glitch during Razorpay checkout, the duplicate amount will be automatically refunded.
    • Technical Errors: If a payment is successfully charged but the team is not registered due to a system crash, a full refund will be processed.

    3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

    • Disqualification: Teams or players disqualified for cheating, hacking, emulator usage violations, toxicity, or breaking official match rules are NOT eligible for any refund.
    • Player No-Show: Failure of a team or player to join the designated match room at the scheduled time will be marked as a forfeit and no refund will be issued.
    • Voluntary Withdrawal: If a team captain decides to withdraw from a tournament after registration has closed, entry fees will not be refunded.

    4. Refund Processing Time

    Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method (Bank Account / UPI / Credit/Debit Card) via Razorpay within 5 to 7 business days.

    5. Contact Support for Refunds

    To request a refund review, please email our support team at support@nexusesports.com with your Razorpay Payment ID and Registration ID.
